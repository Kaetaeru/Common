# Shared Demo Content — VELLUM / ARC 01

Status: LOCKED at content-brief level.

This fictional product is the shared content source for Slide 02 — Visual Design and Slide 03 — Responsive Design. The content remains the same while presentation, layout, and viewport behavior change.

## Why this subject

A sculptural floor lamp is visually neutral enough to support luxury, Swiss, playful, and cinematic art directions without making any one treatment feel like a parody.

It also gives later slides something concrete to demonstrate:

- responsive product layout,
- image cropping and hierarchy changes,
- product controls,
- potential light / dimming interaction,
- product metadata and purchase actions.

The product is fictional. Do not imply a real client relationship or real commercial availability.

## Image / object direction

The same lamp must remain visually recognizable across every style.

Physical design:

- tall, slender floor lamp,
- one continuous shallow aluminium arc,
- small weighted circular base,
- thin integrated light source on the underside of the arc,
- no decorative joints or exposed cables as a focal feature,
- contemporary but believable industrial design,
- available visually in warm silver or graphite.

Required visual asset families when implementation begins:

1. clean isolated product view,
2. quiet interior scene with the lamp illuminated,
3. optional close crop of aluminium surface / light edge.

Do not generate a different lamp for each theme. Different cropping, scale, lighting, and composition are allowed; product identity is not.

## Failure conditions

Revisit this brief if:

- a theme requires changing the product itself to look convincing,
- the content is too long to survive the four-style comparison,
- the lamp becomes visually generic enough that continuity is hard to recognize,

- fictional branding is presented in a way that could be mistaken for a real client case study.
